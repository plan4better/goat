# Style Layers

Now let's customize the appearance of both layers to create a clear map.

## Style the "Supermarkets" Point Layer

1. **Click on the "Supermarkets" layer**: its style panel opens on the right
2. **In the `Points` section, click `Icon`** and turn the icon off with the switch in the editor's header (the points are drawn as simple dots)
3. **Click `Fill`**: if `Color` follows a field, click the button next to it and choose `Fixed`
4. **Choose Orange as the `Color`**

## Style the "Mannheim" Boundary Layer

1. **Click on the "Mannheim" layer**
2. **In the `Polygons` section, click `Fill`** and turn it off with the switch in the editor's header
3. **Click `Outline`** and turn it on the same way
4. **Choose Black as the `Color`** and set the **`Width` to 1**
